# NASA Technical Standard: NDE Requirements for Pressure Vessels (NASA-STD-5009)

## 1. Technical Purpose
Prescribes minimum non-destructive examination (NDE) standards, flaw size detection criteria, and proof pressure testing margins for flight and ground pressure vessels.

## 2. Non-Destructive Examination Criteria
- Ultrasonic Inspection: Calibrated to resolve 0.8 mm Flat Bottom Hole (FBH) artificial flaws in parent metal and welds.
- Proof Testing: Must subject pressure vessels to proof testing factor between 1.25x and 1.50x Maximum Design Pressure (MDP).
- Visual & Surface Inspection: Cracks, lack of fusion, and incomplete penetration flaws are strictly rejectable without allowance.
