# OSHA Guidance Document 3120: The Control of Hazardous Energy (Lockout/Tagout)

## 1. Regulatory Overview
OSHA Standard 29 CFR 1910.147 establishes strict minimum performance requirements for controlling hazardous energy during servicing of machines and industrial equipment.

## 2. Scope & Core Requirements
- Employers must establish an energy control program consisting of written procedures, employee training, and periodic periodic inspections.
- Primary energy isolating devices must be physically capable of being locked out.
- Verification of Isolation: Before starting work, authorized employees must verify that isolation and de-energization of the machine or equipment have been accomplished.
- Tagout Requirements: Tags are warning devices and do not provide the physical restraint of a lock. Where lockout is feasible, lockout MUST be employed.
