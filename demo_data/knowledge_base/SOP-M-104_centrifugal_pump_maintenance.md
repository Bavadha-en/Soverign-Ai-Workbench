# Standard Operating Procedure: Centrifugal Pump Overhaul & Mechanical Seal Maintenance (SOP-M-104)

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP-M-104) defines mandatory maintenance, inspection, and overhaul protocols for single-stage and multi-stage industrial centrifugal pumps (including P-101, P-102, P-201, and P-202 series) handling hydrocarbons, cooling water, and slurry. Conforms to ISO 10816-3 Class II and API 610 11th Edition.

## 2. Safety Precautions & Energy Isolation
- Mandatory Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) per SOP-SAF-001. De-energize 415V/3.3kV motor supply breaker.
- Close, chain, and car-seal suction and discharge isolation valves.
- Depressurize casing via high-point vent; drain process fluid to closed drain header.
- Ensure casing skin temperature is below 40°C before bolt loosening.

## 3. Vibration & Condition Monitoring Thresholds (ISO 10816-3 Class II)
Triaxial velocity vibration measurements must be logged on pump and motor bearing housings (horizontal, vertical, axial):
- **Zone A / Good (< 1.8 mm/s RMS)**: Newly commissioned or precision-rebuilt machine.
- **Zone B / Acceptable (1.8 - 4.5 mm/s RMS)**: Normal continuous industrial operation.
- **Zone C / Warning (4.5 - 7.1 mm/s RMS)**: Elevated vibration indicating bearing degradation, cavitation, or unbalance. Schedule planned overhaul within 14 days. Increase condition logging to daily.
- **Zone D / Critical Trip (> 7.1 mm/s RMS)**: Extreme vibration indicating severe mechanical damage, impeller detachment, or shaft bending. Mandates IMMEDIATE EMERGENCY SHUTDOWN and isolation.

## 4. Mechanical Seal & Bearing Inspection Criteria
- Inspect cartridge mechanical seal faces (Silicon Carbide vs. Tungsten Carbide).
- Maximum allowable seal face runout: 0.025 mm. Face flatness must be within 2 helium light bands (0.58 micrometers).
- Dynamic seal leakage limit: Greater than 5 drops/minute (10 ml/hour) requires immediate seal replacement.
- Inspect deep-groove ball bearings and angular contact thrust bearings. Radial play must not exceed 0.040 mm.
- Bearing temperature must not exceed 75°C under continuous full-load operating conditions.

## 5. Shaft Alignment & Soft Foot Tolerances
- Laser alignment tolerances at operating temperature:
  - Angular misalignment: <= 0.05 mm per 100 mm coupling diameter.
  - Parallel offset: <= 0.05 mm (50 micrometers).
- Maximum permissible soft foot: 0.05 mm at each mounting foot before final torque sequence (180 Nm).

## 6. Hydrostatic Re-Commissioning & Acceptance
- Hydrostatic test casing at 1.5x Maximum Allowable Working Pressure (MAWP) for 15 minutes without pressure drop.
- Rotate shaft 360 degrees by hand to confirm smooth rotation without binding or rubbing.
- Execute 2-hour continuous baseline run; record vibration, bearing temperature, and seal chamber pressure.
