# Standard Operating Procedure: Process Piping System Corrosion Inspection (SOP-PIP-405)

## 1. Scope & Standards
Specifies external visual examination, Corrosion Under Insulation (CUI) stripping, and ultrasonic thickness grid measurements for process piping circuits per API 570 and ASME B31.3.

## 2. Corrosion Under Insulation (CUI) High-Risk Zones
- Operating temperature ranges of -4°C to 120°C (thermal cycling zones where moisture condensation concentrates).
- Insulation terminations, penetrations, hangers, flanges, and piping low points.
- Strip insulation jackets and conduct 100% visual inspection of external pipe metal surface.

## 3. Ultrasonic Wall Thickness Monitoring
- Establish Condition Monitoring Locations (CMLs) at elbows, reducers, tees, and downstream of orifice plates.
- Minimum structural thickness calculation per ASME B31.3:
  t_min = (P * D) / (2 * (S * E + P * Y))
- If measured thickness approaches t_min + 0.5 mm, execute immediate fitness-for-service assessment and fabricate temporary ASME-compliant piping clamp or schedule spool replacement.
