# Standard Operating Procedure: Hazardous Energy Control - Lockout/Tagout (SOP-SAF-001)

## 1. Purpose & Compliance
Mandates procedural requirements for isolating electrical, mechanical, hydraulic, pneumatic, and thermal energy sources to prevent accidental re-energization during maintenance per OSHA 29 CFR 1910.147.

## 2. Seven-Step Lockout/Tagout Procedure
1. **Notify Affected Employees**: Inform plant operations personnel before commencing lockout.
2. **Shutdown Equipment**: Execute normal operational stop sequence from DCS/local control station.
3. **Isolate Energy Sources**: Open primary motor circuit breaker, racking out disconnect switches.
4. **Apply Lockout and Tagout Devices**: Place red Master Safety Lock and standardized danger tag.
5. **Release Stored Energy**: Bleed residual hydraulic pressure, drain liquid headers, discharge capacitors.
6. **Zero-Energy State Verification**: Perform 'Try-Step' by attempting local start; verify zero voltage using calibrated multimeter.
7. **Commence Maintenance**: Secure personal safety locks on group lockout hasp.
