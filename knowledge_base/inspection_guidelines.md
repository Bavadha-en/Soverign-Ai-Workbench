# Industrial Pipeline & Equipment Corrosion Inspection Guidelines

## 1. Visual and Ultrasonic Inspection Criteria
- Inspect pipe runs, elbows, and weld seams for pitting corrosion, galvanic degradation, and erosion.
- Measure pit depth with optical pit gauges and record minimum remaining wall thickness ($t_{\text{min}}$).

## 2. Critical Thresholds & Risk Categorization
- **CRITICAL / HIGH**: Wall thickness reduction $> 35\%$, or active flange leakage. Immediate isolation and replacement required.
- **MEDIUM**: Wall thinning between $20\% - 35\%$. Schedule planned maintenance within 30 days.
- **LOW / ACCEPTABLE**: Wall thinning $< 20\%$. Continue standard 6-month inspection cycle.

## 3. Centrifugal Pump Seal Degradation
- Inspect mechanical seal face wear on pumps (P-401 series).
- Replace elastomer O-rings and tungsten carbide seal faces if leakage rate exceeds 5 drops/minute.
