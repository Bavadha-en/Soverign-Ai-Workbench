# Standard Operating Procedure: Centrifugal Pump Overhaul & Mechanical Seal Maintenance (SOP-M-104)

## 1. Scope & Objective
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP-M-104) defines the mandatory technical guidelines and safety steps for vibration diagnosis, precision shaft alignment, mechanical seal overhaul, and casing integrity inspection of industrial centrifugal slurry and cooling water pumps (P-401 and P-402 series).

## 2. Safety Precautions & Hazardous Energy Isolation (LOTO)
- Apply full electrical Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) on pump motor circuit breaker in accordance with SOP-SAF-001.
- Close, chain, and lock suction and discharge isolation valves.
- Depressurize and drain pump casing into hazardous chemical collection sumps.
- Verify zero differential pressure and ambient temperature (< 40°C) before loosening casing bolts.
- Mandatory PPE: Safety glasses with side shields, chemical-resistant nitrile gloves, steel-toe boots, and hearing protection.

## 3. Vibration & Dynamic Operational Thresholds (ISO 10816-3 Class II)
Prior to shutdown, perform triaxial vibration analysis on pump and motor bearing housings:
- **Zone A / Good (< 1.8 mm/s RMS)**: Newly commissioned or completely overhauled machinery.
- **Zone B / Acceptable (1.8 – 4.5 mm/s RMS)**: Normal continuous operation with no restrictions.
- **Zone C / Warning (4.5 – 7.1 mm/s RMS)**: Significant deterioration; schedule planned mechanical overhaul within 14 days.
- **Zone D / Critical Trip (> 7.1 mm/s RMS)**: Extreme risk of catastrophic impeller or seal failure; trigger immediate emergency shutdown.

## 4. Mechanical Seal Inspection & Overhaul Criteria
- Dismantle cartridge mechanical seal assembly. Inspect silicon carbide (SiC) and tungsten carbide (TC) seal faces.
- Measure face flatness using monochromatic light and optical flat; flatness must be within 2 helium light bands (0.58 μm).
- Replace all fluorocarbon (FKM/Viton) O-rings, secondary elastomeric packings, and spring assemblies during every maintenance cycle.
- Reject seal faces exhibiting thermal heat checking, micro-cracks, pitting, or blistering.
- Operating leakage threshold: If dynamic leakage exceeds 5 drops/minute (approx. 10 ml/hour), replace seal assembly immediately.

## 5. Precision Laser Shaft Alignment Tolerances
- Clean and deburr all motor and pump mounting feet. Check for soft foot condition (maximum allowable soft foot: 0.05 mm).
- Align pump and motor shafts using dual-laser alignment equipment:
  - **Angular Misalignment**: Must not exceed 0.05 mm per 100 mm coupling diameter (0.05°).
  - **Parallel Radial Offset**: Must not exceed 0.05 mm (50 μm) at operating temperature.
- Torque motor hold-down bolts to 180 Nm in alternating diagonal cross sequence.

## 6. Post-Maintenance Hydrostatic & Performance Re-Commissioning
- Perform casing hydrostatic pressure test at 1.5x Maximum Allowable Working Pressure (MAWP) for 15 minutes.
- Verify zero pressure drop and zero gasket weeping.
- Fill casing, bleed entrapped air via casing vent valve, rotate shaft by hand to confirm free rotation.
- Re-energize motor and execute 2-hour continuous run test; log vibration, bearing temperature (< 80°C), and seal chamber pressure.
