# Standard Operating Procedure: Shell-and-Tube Heat Exchanger Tube Inspection & Plugging (SOP-HEX-205)

## 1. Scope & Objective
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP-HEX-205) establishes the mandatory technical requirements for conducting non-destructive eddy current testing (ECT), internal rotary inspection (IRIS), tube plugging, and hydrostatic shell/tube bundle leak testing on TEMA Type AES/BEM shell-and-tube heat exchangers (E-100 series).

## 2. Safety Precautions & Environmental Protection
- Apply full Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) on tube-side and shell-side fluid isolation valves.
- High pressure hydro-jetting cleaning: Establish 10-meter perimeter exclusion zone with warning barriers and splash shields.
- Technicians operating hydro-jetting equipment (10,000 – 15,000 PSI) must wear Kevlar-reinforced protective suits, metatarsal guards, and full-face shields.
- Neutralize and collect all chemical cleaning effluents in designated industrial wastewater containment basins.

## 3. Eddy Current Testing (ECT) & IRIS Inspection Criteria
- Inspect 100% of exchanger tubes in peripheral rows and a minimum of 20% random sampling across the core bundle using multi-frequency ECT probes.
- Utilize Internal Rotary Inspection System (IRIS) ultrasonic probes for ferrous and thick-walled exchanger tubes.
- Measure localized wall thinning, baffle wear cuts, steam impingement erosion, and stress corrosion cracking.

## 4. Tube Degradation Thresholds & Remediation Action Matrix
- **Category 1 (Wall Loss < 20%)**: Minor thinning; tube is structurally sound. Retain in service without action.
- **Category 2 (Wall Loss 20% – 40%)**: Moderate thinning; flag tube location in bundle map. Schedule re-examination during next turnaround (within 24 months).
- **Category 3 (Wall Loss > 40% or Through-Wall Pinhole Leak)**: Severe integrity breach. Mandatory mechanical tube plugging before returning unit to operation.

## 5. Mechanical Tube Plugging Protocol
- Clean tube ends and tube-sheet holes to bare metal using rotary brass wire brushes.
- Install ASME PCC-2 approved tapered mechanical friction plugs or explosive weld plugs:
  - Match plug metallurgy to tube metallurgy (e.g., Titanium Gr. 2, Monel 400, or 316L SS) to eliminate galvanic corrosion risks.
  - Torque mechanical friction plugs to manufacturer specified torque (typically 45–60 Nm).
- **Maximum Cumulative Plugging Limit**: Total plugged tubes must not exceed 10% of total bundle tube count. If plugged tubes exceed 10%, thermal performance rating drops below design threshold and total bundle retubing/replacement is mandatory.

## 6. Hydrostatic Differential Testing & Return to Service
- Mount test ring on exchanger shell and apply hydrostatic test pressure on shell side (1.5x design shell pressure) for 60 minutes.
- Visually inspect tube-sheet faces for weeping or seepage around plugged and unplugged tubes.
- Install channel cover with new corrugated metal graphite-faced gaskets. Torque flange studs in crisscross star sequence to 320 Nm.
- Perform final tube-side hydrostatic proof test at 1.5x design tube pressure.
