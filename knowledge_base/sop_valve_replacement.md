# Standard Operating Procedure: High Pressure Control Valve Replacement (SOP-M-402)

## 1. Scope & Objective
This procedure specifies the mandatory safety and technical steps for isolating, removing, and replacing severely corroded high-pressure control valves (such as CV-102) in industrial refining and chemical transport pipelines.

## 2. Safety Precautions & Lockout/Tagout (LOTO)
- Ensure complete line depressurization and cool-down to ambient temperature before unbolting.
- Apply double block and bleed (DBB) isolation on upstream and downstream isolation valves.
- Verify zero residual pressure using calibrated pressure gauges.
- Mandatory PPE: chemical splash goggles, heat-resistant gloves, and full protective coveralls.

## 3. Disassembly and Wall Thickness Inspection
- Measure remaining wall thickness on adjacent pipe flanges using ultrasonic testing (UT).
- If wall loss exceeds 30%, replace adjacent spool pieces per ASME B31.3 standards.
- Discard old gaskets; never reuse spiral wound or RTJ metallic gaskets.

## 4. Installation & Hydrostatic Testing
- Install certified 316L stainless steel replacement control valve with Class 600 RTJ flanges.
- Torque bolts in cross-pattern star sequence to 220 Nm.
- Perform hydrostatic pressure testing up to 1.5x maximum operating pressure for 30 minutes with zero detectable pressure drop.
